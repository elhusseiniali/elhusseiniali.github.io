I am a PhD candidate in the Programming Languages and Software Engineering (PLSE) lab at the National
University of Singapore, advised by [Prof. Abhik Roychoudhury](https://abhikrc.com) and
[Prof. Blaise Genest](https://perso.crans.org/genest/), as part of the [Descartes](https://descartes.cnrsatcreate.cnrs.fr/)
program. My research focuses on improving the security of large language models (and the agentic
systems built on top of them) by understanding and analyzing how these models operate.

Before NUS, I completed the [Parisian Masters of Research in Computer Science](https://wikimpri.dptinfo.ens-cachan.fr/doku.php?id=start)
(MPRI) at the Ecole Normale Superieure, and a BS in Computer Science and BA in Philosophy at the
American University of Beirut.
